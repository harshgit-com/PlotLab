Global automatic rules apply to eligible movies. Per-movie settings can override the schedule. Both global enablement and verified movie rights are required.

Production ads stay inactive until a valid HTTPS VAST tag is provided.

Automatic policy: under 30 min = no mid-roll; 30–59 min = one near halfway; 60+ min = up to two near one-third and two-thirds. The opening 15 and final 10 minutes are protected. Custom positions override this schedule.
