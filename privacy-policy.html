Effective date: 28 November 2026 · Last updated: 28 November 2026

PlotLab (“PlotLab”, “we”, “us” or “our”) values privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you visit or use the PlotLab website, including its film-discovery and creator-community features. Please read it before creating an account or submitting information.

1. Who is responsible?

PlotLab is currently presented as an independently operated online project. It is not represented on this page as an incorporated company. 2. Information we may collect

  • Account information: name, email address, account identifiers and authentication details handled through the sign-in provider.
  • Creator profile information: information you choose to submit, such as profile photo, city, creator role, portfolio or audition material.
  • Community information: connection requests, messages, reviews, ratings and other material you submit through available features.
  • Usage and device information: pages or features used, basic device/browser information, interaction events, and technical logs used to operate, protect and improve the service.
  • Local browser storage: PlotLab may use browser storage for preferences and features such as watch-later lists, viewing progress and recently viewed items.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless a feature specifically requires it and you understand why it is needed. Do not upload another person’s photograph, personal information or work without their permission.

3. Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; provide film discovery and playback features; display creator profiles; enable connections and messaging; store preferences; respond to support, privacy and legal requests; prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents; maintain service analytics; and comply with applicable law.

4. Legal basis, consent and your choices

Where applicable under Indian law, we will process digital personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules and commencement notifications applicable to the relevant processing at the time. Where consent is required, it should be specific, informed, unambiguous and given through a clear affirmative action. You may withdraw consent by contacting us; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out lawfully before withdrawal. Some features may stop working if the information required to provide them is deleted or consent is withdrawn.

5. Service providers and sharing

PlotLab uses or may use third-party services to operate features. These include Google/Firebase for authentication, database and related infrastructure; Cloudinary for media delivery; YouTube or other media hosts for embedded trailers; Vercel for website hosting; and Google advertising services if and when advertising is enabled. These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information as a business practice. We may disclose information where required by law, valid legal process, to protect users or the service, or as part of a legitimate business reorganisation.

6. Advertising, cookies and similar technologies

PlotLab may use essential browser storage to remember your preferences and may use third-party cookies or similar technologies if advertising or analytics services are enabled. If Google AdSense or another advertising provider is activated, that provider may use cookies or device identifiers to serve, measure and personalise ads, subject to its own policies and your available settings. Advertising is not guaranteed to be active merely because this policy mentions it. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may affect some features.

7. Storage, security and retention

Information may be stored by PlotLab and its service providers on servers located in India or other jurisdictions, subject to applicable law and provider safeguards. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, account operation, dispute resolution, security, legal compliance and enforcement of our terms. Retention periods can vary by data type and provider.

8. Your requests and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access information associated with you, correct or update it, request deletion, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or raise a grievance. Send requests from your account email to plotlab29@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request”. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Certain information may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it.

9. Children

PlotLab is not designed to knowingly collect personal data from children in violation of applicable Indian law. If you are a parent or lawful guardian and believe a child has provided personal data without the required authorisation, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate steps. Do not create an account or upload a child’s personal information or images unless the required legal conditions and verifiable parental consent have been met.

10. Third-party websites and media

Links, embedded trailers and externally hosted media may take you to services not controlled by PlotLab. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Review those policies before using the external service.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as PlotLab’s features, service providers or legal obligations change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the website where appropriate.
